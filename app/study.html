📧 Informal Letter / Email (to a friend)

Opening

Dear [Name],  /  Hi [Name],
How are you? I hope you and your family are doing well. It was such a pleasure to receive your last letter — thank you for keeping in touch! I'm really sorry for not writing sooner, but I've been quite busy.

Purpose sentence

I'm writing this letter to tell you about... / I wanted to let you know that... / I'm writing to invite you to...

Body connectors

By the way, ...  ·  Anyway, ...  ·  I almost forgot to mention...  ·  On another note, ...

Closing

I'd better get going now. Please send my warmest regards to your family. Looking forward to hearing from you soon!
Best wishes, / Love, / Yours,

📬 Formal Letter — General Structure

Opening

Dear Sir/Madam,  /  Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],
I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to [state purpose].

Purpose phrases by type

  • Inquiry: I would appreciate it if you could provide me with further details regarding...
  • Request: I would be grateful if you could assist me with... / I would like to request permission to...
  • Inform: I am writing to inform you that... / I wish to bring to your attention...
  • Complaint: I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with... / I would like to draw your attention to a problem I experienced...
  • Apologise: I sincerely apologise for [issue] and would like to offer a solution...
  • Application: I am writing to apply for the position of... / I am interested in attending the training course on...

Closing

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to your response at your earliest convenience. Please feel free to contact me should you need any further information.
Yours faithfully, (if opened with Dear Sir/Madam)  /  Yours sincerely, (if opened with a name)

✉️ Formal Letter — Complaint (expanded)

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with [product/service] that I [purchased/experienced] on [date].

Firstly, [describe the main problem clearly]. To make matters worse, [second issue]. As a result, [impact on you].

I would be grateful if you could [suggested solution — refund / replacement / apology]. I expect this matter to be resolved within [timeframe].

I look forward to your prompt response.

Yours faithfully,
[Your name]

📩 Formal Letter — Request / Application

Dear Mr./Ms. [Name],

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to request [permission / information / assistance] regarding [topic].

The reason I am making this request is that [explain context and need]. This would be extremely beneficial because [reason 1]. Furthermore, [reason 2].

I would therefore be grateful if you could [specific action]. I am happy to provide any additional information you may require.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]

🔵 Type 1 — Discussion / Opinion / Advantages & Disadvantages

Introduction

In recent years, [Topic] has become a widely debated issue, not only on a global scale but also in Vietnam. While some view it as a positive development, others argue that it presents significant challenges. This essay will shed light on both perspectives before reaching a conclusion. / Personally, I strongly [agree/disagree] with this viewpoint, and the following paragraphs will explain my reasons.

Body 1 — Advantages / For

On the one hand, [Topic] is beneficial in several ways. The most obvious merit is that [point 1]. For example, [example]. In the second place, [point 2]. To illustrate, [detail]. Last but not least, [point 3].

Body 2 — Disadvantages / Against

On the other hand, the disadvantages of [Topic] are undeniable. First and foremost, [problem 1]. For example, [example]. The second drawback is that [problem 2]. The final disadvantage is that [problem 3].

Conclusion

All in all, [Topic] has both benefits and drawbacks, but its impact depends on how it is managed. By adopting a balanced approach, we can maximise benefits and minimise risks, ensuring that [Topic] contributes positively to society. / From my perspective, I firmly believe that [personal view] because [reason].

🟠 Type 2 — Cause & Effect

Introduction

In recent years, [Topic] has become an increasingly urgent issue, drawing widespread public concern. This essay will explore the primary causes behind this phenomenon and assess its potential consequences.

Body 1 — Causes

There are several factors leading to [Topic]. The first and foremost cause is that [cause 1]. To be more specific, [explanation]. Another contributing factor is that [cause 2]. To illustrate, [example]. Last but not least, [cause 3]. For example, [detail].

Body 2 — Effects

As a consequence, [Topic] has resulted in several notable impacts. First and foremost, [effect 1]. To illustrate, [detail]. Furthermore, [effect 2]. As a result, [further consequence]. Finally, [effect 3].

Conclusion

All in all, [Topic] is mainly caused by [cause 1] and [cause 2], leading to [effect 1] and [effect 2]. If no action is taken, this issue will continue to worsen in the future. Therefore, raising awareness and implementing preventive measures are essential to mitigating its negative effects.

🟢 Type 3 — Cause & Solution

Introduction

In recent years, [Topic] has emerged as a growing concern, sparking widespread discussions on effective ways to tackle it. This essay will delve into the underlying causes of this problem and suggest practical solutions.

Body 1 — Causes

There are several factors leading to [Topic]. The first and foremost cause is that [cause 1]. To be more specific, [detail]. Another factor is that [cause 2]. To illustrate, [example]. Last but not least, [cause 3].

Body 2 — Solutions

There are various methods which could be taken to tackle the problem of [Topic]. The first measure is that [solution 1]. As a result, [expected outcome]. Another approach is [solution 2]. As a consequence, [benefit]. Finally, [solution 3] would also prove effective.

Conclusion

All in all, [Topic] is mainly caused by [cause 1] and [cause 2], but this issue can be effectively addressed by implementing [solution 1] and [solution 2]. If appropriate measures are put in place, this situation will improve significantly in the near future.

🔴 Type 4 — Problem & Solution

Introduction

One of the most pressing issues facing society today is [Topic]. This essay will examine the main problems associated with [Topic] and propose realistic solutions to address them.

Body 1 — Problems

The most significant problem is [problem 1]. This is because [reason/evidence]. A further issue is [problem 2], which leads to [negative consequence]. Moreover, [problem 3] is a serious concern that cannot be overlooked.

Body 2 — Solutions

Fortunately, several steps can be taken to address these issues. To begin with, [solution 1] would help by [explanation]. In addition, [solution 2] is another effective measure because [reason]. Finally, [solution 3] could be implemented by [authority/individual], which would lead to [positive outcome].

Conclusion

In conclusion, while [Topic] presents several serious challenges, these can be overcome through [summary of solutions]. It is hoped that by taking these steps, we will create a better and more [adjective] society for future generations.
➕ Adding Points
  • Furthermore / Moreover / In addition / What is more — used to add a similar or stronger point
  • Not only … but also — e.g. Not only does it save time, but it also reduces costs.
  • Besides / On top of that / Apart from this — informal-to-neutral additions
  • Last but not least — final point that is equally important
↔️ Contrasting
  • However / Nevertheless / Nonetheless — introduce an opposite idea
  • On the other hand / On the contrary — present the opposing side
  • Despite / In spite of — e.g. Despite the benefits, there are serious risks.
  • Although / Even though / While / Whereas — within one sentence
  • In contrast / By contrast — compare two different things
📌 Giving Examples
  • For example / For instance — most common
  • Such as / Including — used within a list
  • To illustrate / To be more specific — more formal
  • A clear illustration of this is… / This can be seen in… — evidence-based
  • In Vietnam, for example, … — localised example (great for VSTEP)
⚡ Cause & Effect
  • As a result / As a consequence / Therefore / Consequently — show outcome
  • This leads to / This results in / This causes — direct causation
  • Due to / Owing to / Because of — introduce cause
  • Since / Because / As — within a sentence
  • If … then … / Unless … — conditional cause-effect
🔚 Concluding
  • In conclusion / To conclude / To sum up / In summary — open the conclusion
  • All in all / All things considered / Taking everything into account — balanced summary
  • It is hoped that… / It is clear that… — optimistic/factual close
  • Therefore, I strongly believe that… — opinion restatement
💭 Giving Opinion
  • In my opinion / From my perspective / Personally, I think — direct opinion
  • I strongly believe that / I am firmly convinced that — strong opinion
  • It seems to me that / I would argue that — softer / academic
  • From my point of view / As far as I am concerned — formal alternatives
📈 Degree / Emphasis
  • Undeniably / Evidently / Obviously / Clearly — state the obvious strongly
  • To a great extent / To some extent — partial agreement
  • Above all / Most importantly / In particular — highlight key point
  • It is worth noting that… / It should be pointed out that… — draw attention
🗣️ Speaking — Useful Expressions
  • Well, that's a good question. Let me think about it… — buying time
  • From my experience, … / In my daily life, … — personal connection
  • I feel strongly that… / I'm inclined to think that… — expressing views
  • What I mean is… / In other words, … — clarifying
  • As I mentioned earlier, … / Getting back to… — referring back
  • On balance, I would say that… — balanced conclusion
